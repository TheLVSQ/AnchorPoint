This guide is for volunteers. It shows you how to sign in, answer when you're asked to
serve, and tell your team leader which weeks you serve and when you're away. Each step takes
about **a minute**, and it all works on your phone.

## When you're asked to serve

When a leader schedules you, you get an **email** listing your dates, with a link to answer.
You don't need to sign in to use the link.

1. Open the email and tap the link.
2. For each date, tap **Accept**, or tap **Decline**.
3. If you decline, you can add a reason (optional), then tap **Decline** with the date to
   confirm.

![Your dates, opened from the email link](serving/v03-respond.png)

When you decline, your leader gets an email straight away so they can find someone else.

Changed your mind? Open the link again: tap **Can't make it now** on a date you accepted, or
**I can serve after all** on one you declined. You can change your answer until the day
itself.

Three days before each date you'll get a **reminder email**.

!!! tip "Not getting emails?"
    Check your inbox's spam or promotions folder. If you clicked **Unsubscribe** at the
    bottom of a church email, you won't get these either. Ask your leader to turn church
    emails back on for you.

## Signing in for the first time

If your leader invites you to log in, you get an email titled *You're invited to* (your
church's name), with a link to set your password.

1. Tap the link in the email. It works once and lasts 3 days.
2. Type a new password twice.
3. Tap **Set password and sign in**.

![Setting your password from the invite email](serving/v02-set-password.png)

After that, sign in with your **email address** and that password:

![The sign-in page](serving/v01-login.png)

**Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, type your email, and
we'll send you a new link.

If the invite link has expired, ask your leader to click **Resend invite**.

## Your home page

When you sign in, anything waiting for your answer shows at the top. Tap it to go to
**My serving**.

![Waiting for your answer, at the top of your home page](serving/v04-dashboard.png)

## My serving

Tap **My serving** in the menu. It has three parts.

### My schedule

All your upcoming dates and your answer for each:

- **Needs your answer**: tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **Serving**: you said yes.
- **Can't make it**: you said no. Tap **I can serve after all** if that changes.

![My schedule: every upcoming date and your answer](serving/v05-my-schedule.png)

Tap **Songs** under a date to see what's being played: each song's key and notes, its
**chord chart** (opens the PDF) and any **rehearsal links**. You can open the charts until the date
has passed.

![The songs for a date, with charts and rehearsal links](serving/v10-setlist.png)

Tap **Who's serving with you** under a date to see who else on your team is serving that day.

When you decline, you can say why. Your leader sees your reason.

![Declining with a reason](serving/v06-decline.png)

### My teams

Each team you're on, the positions you play, and the **weeks of the month you serve**. If you
only serve the 3rd Sunday, untick the other weeks and tap **Save**. Your leader sees this when
building the schedule.

![The weeks you serve on each team](serving/v07-weeks.png)

Your positions (like Keys or Vocals) are set by your leader. Ask them if one is missing.

### Who's on your team, and who sees your number

Tap **Who's on the team** to see everyone on that team and their positions. You can tap a phone
number or email to call, text or write to them.

![Your team, with the phone and email they share](serving/v09-team.png)

At the bottom of **My teams**, choose who can see **your** phone and email, then tap **Save**:

- **People on my teams** (the usual choice): everyone on a team with you.
- **Team leaders only**: just the leaders of your teams.

!!! note "Children"
    A child's or teen's phone and email are never shown, whatever is chosen. Their name still
    shows so you know who's on the team.

### Dates I'm away

Going to be away? Add the dates here, and your leader sees it before scheduling you.

1. Pick the **From** date. For more than one day, pick the **To** date too.
2. Tick **Every year** for dates that repeat, like a yearly trip.
3. Add a note if you like (*Vacation*), then tap **Add blockout**.

![Adding dates you're away](serving/v08-away.png)

Dates you're away count for every team you're on. Tap **Remove** next to one if your plans
change.

!!! note "Away for a date you already said yes to?"
    Adding dates you're away doesn't cancel a date you already accepted. Go to
    **My schedule** and tap **Can't make it now** on that date too.
