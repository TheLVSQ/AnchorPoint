This guide shows you how to schedule volunteers in AnchorPoint: set up a team, build a
schedule, and send people their dates so they can accept or decline. Setting up the first
team and service takes about **20 minutes**. After that, filling a month of Sundays takes
about **5 minutes**.

!!! note "Before you start"
    - **Your role:** You need a **Staff** or **Admin** login. If you don't see **Schedules**
      in the sidebar, ask an Admin.
    - **The team exists under Groups**, with its people added as members. Any volunteer team
      works (worship, kids, greeters, tech). Check-in classrooms and event groups can't be
      scheduled.
    - **Volunteers need an email address** on their profile to get their dates. Everyone else
      can still be scheduled; you just tell them yourself.

## How it fits together

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Position** | A job on a team: Keys, Vocals, Sound, Nursery helper. |
| **Service type** | Something you schedule for again and again, like *Sunday Morning* or *VBS 2027*. It says how many of each position you need. |
| **Plan** | One date of a service type, like Sunday, Nov 8. |
| **Request** | The email someone gets listing their dates, with a link to accept or decline. |

You set up the team once, create the service type once, then fill in dates and send requests
as often as you need.

## Step 1: Add positions to the team

1. Go to **Groups** and open the team.
2. In the **Positions** card, type a position name (for example *Keys*) and click **Add**.
3. Repeat for every job on the team.

![The Positions card on a team's page, with Guitar, Keys, Sound and Vocals](serving/s01-positions.png)

!!! tip "Removing a position"
    You can only remove a position nobody is scheduled in. If someone is, AnchorPoint tells
    you which dates to clear first.

## Step 2: Set what each person does and when

For each member of the team:

1. Click **Edit** next to their name in the **Members** list.
2. Tick the **Positions** they can fill.
3. Under **Serves on**, tick the weeks of the month they serve. Leave all five ticked for
   *every week*. Tick only **3rd** for someone who serves the 3rd Sunday of each month.
4. Click **Save**.

![Editing one team member: role, positions, and the weeks they serve](serving/s02-member-edit.png)

The weeks follow the calendar: the 1st–7th of the month is the 1st week, the 8th–14th the
2nd week, and so on. Volunteers can change their own weeks later, but only you can change
their positions.

## Step 3: Invite volunteers to log in (optional)

A login lets a volunteer see all their dates, answer requests, and say when they're away.
Without one, they can still answer from the link in each email.

1. Open the person's profile (**People**, then their name).
2. In the **Login** card, click **Invite to log in**.

![The Login card on a person's profile, with Invite to log in](serving/s03-invite.png)

They get an email with a link to set a password. The link works once and expires after 3
days. If it expires, the button changes to **Resend invite**.

Volunteers who log in only see their own dashboard, profile, schedule and these help
pages. They can't see the directory or anyone's children.

On **My serving** they also see who's on their teams and who else is serving on their dates.
Each person's phone and email show to **People on my teams** (the default) or **Team leaders
only**. Volunteers choose this themselves, or you can set it under **Phone and email visible to**
when you edit their profile. Anyone under 18, in a school grade, or listed as a child or student
in a family never has their phone or email shown.

## Step 4: Create a service type

1. Go to **Schedules** and click **New service type**.
2. Give it a **Name**, like *Sunday Morning*.
3. Pick the **Weekday** it happens on. For something that runs several days in a row (like
   VBS), pick **Every day**.
4. Set the **Service time**.
5. Under **People needed each date**, type how many of each position you need. Leave **0**
   for positions this service doesn't use.
6. Click **Create**.

![A service type: name, weekday, time, and how many of each position each date needs](serving/s05-service-type.png)

You can change these numbers any time with **Edit positions** on the schedule.

## Step 5: Add dates

At the bottom of the schedule, pick a **From** and **To** date and click **Add dates**. You
get one plan for each Sunday (or each day, for an *Every day* service type) in that range.
Dates that already exist are skipped, so it's safe to run again.

![Add dates: pick a range and AnchorPoint makes a plan for each date](serving/s07-add-dates.png)

## Step 6: Fill in the schedule

The schedule shows positions down the side and dates across the top. Each square shows
who's in it and how many you still need: **0 of 2** turns orange while it's short.

![The schedule: positions down the side, dates across the top](serving/s06-grid.png)

To put someone in a spot:

1. Click **+ Add** in the square.
2. Pick a person from the list. People who can fill that position come first. Under
   **Others on the team** are members who aren't marked for it.
3. Click **Done** when you've finished with that square.

![Picking someone: the list shows anything that might be a problem](serving/s09-picker.png)

AnchorPoint warns you (⚠) when someone:

- serves **other weeks** (for example *Serves 3rd only*),
- is **blocked out** that day (they said they're away), or
- is **already serving** something else that day.

These are warnings, not rules. If you pick someone with a warning, AnchorPoint asks
*Schedule anyway?* first. A warning also shows up later if someone adds a blockout after you
scheduled them.

To take someone off, click the **×** next to their name.

!!! tip "Use one team at a time"
    If several teams share a service type, click a team name above the schedule to show only
    that team. **Send requests** then only sends that team's dates, so another team's
    half-finished schedule stays put.

## Step 7: Send requests

New people show **Not sent** until you send them. Only staff see them, so you can move people
around freely.

When the schedule looks right, click **Send requests** at the top. The number on the button
is how many people will get an email.

![Send requests: the number is how many people get an email](serving/s08-send.png)

Within a minute, each person gets **one** email listing all their new dates, with a link to
accept or decline. If someone has **No email** next to their name, AnchorPoint tells you who
couldn't be emailed. Let them know another way.

## Step 8: Follow the answers

Each name shows where things stand:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not sent** | Scheduled, but you haven't sent the request yet. |
| **Pending** | Request sent; they haven't answered. |
| **Accepted** | They're coming. |
| **Declined** | They can't. Their name is crossed out and doesn't count toward the spot. Hover over **Declined** to see their reason. |
| **No email** | No email address, or they turned church emails off. Tell them yourself. |

When someone declines, **you get an email** with their reason and a link to that date, so you
can find someone else.

Three days before each date, everyone who hasn't declined gets a **reminder email**. Reminders
go out after 8am.

## Songs and setlists

### Add songs to the library

1. Go to **Songs** and click **New song**.
2. Type the **Title**, the **Writers**, and the **CCLI song number** (from SongSelect). Click
   **Add song**.
3. On the song's page, click **Edit** on the **Default** arrangement to set its **Key** and **BPM**.
4. Under **Charts**, give the chart a **Label** (for example *Chord chart*), choose the PDF, and click
   **Upload chart**.
5. Under **Rehearsal links**, add a YouTube or Spotify link if you have one.

![A song: its key and tempo, chart PDFs, and rehearsal links](serving/s10-song.png)

Charts must be PDFs of 20 MB or less. If your team plays a song two ways (say, an acoustic version
in a lower key), click **Add arrangement**.

Songs you don't use any more: click **Archive song**. It disappears from the library but stays on
past dates.

### Put songs on a date

1. Open the date: click it at the top of the schedule.
2. Under **Songs**, type part of a song's title and click **Add**.
3. Use the arrows to change the order, and **×** to take a song off.
4. Playing it in a different key that day? Click **Key and notes**, type the key, and click **Save**.

![The songs for one date, with the song search below](serving/s11-setlist.png)

Everyone scheduled that date sees the songs, keys, notes, charts and links on **My serving** once
their request has been sent (not while they show **Not sent**, and not if they declined). They can
open the charts until the date has passed. Charts never go out in emails, and volunteers can't
browse the whole library.

## Team leaders can schedule their own team

You don't have to build every schedule yourself. Anyone marked **Leader** on a team can schedule
that team, even without a Staff login:

1. Go to **Groups**, open the team, and click **Edit** next to the leader.
2. Set **Role** to **Leader** and click **Save**.
3. Make sure they can log in (see [Step 3](#step-3-invite-volunteers-to-log-in-optional)).

They then see **Schedules** and **Songs** in their menu. On a schedule they only see their own
team's positions, and they can add and remove people and click **Send requests** for their team.
When someone declines a date the leader scheduled, the leader gets the email. Leaders can also
add songs to the library and choose the songs for dates their team serves on.

Leaders **can't** create service types, change how many people a date needs, add or delete
dates, or open anyone's profile. Staff still do those.

## Dates someone is away

Volunteers can add the dates they're away themselves. You can also add them for someone: open
their profile, find the **Serving** card, and use **Add a blockout**. Tick **Every year** for
dates that repeat, like a yearly trip.

![The Serving card on a profile: their teams, upcoming dates, and blockouts](serving/s04-person-serving.png)

A blockout applies to every team the person is on.

## Common questions

**Someone says they never got the email.**
Check their profile has the right email address, and that **Email opt in** is still ticked.
The **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom of every church email turns it off. They can still
answer from **My serving** if they have a login.

**I took someone off a date after their request went out.**
AnchorPoint doesn't tell them yet. Let them know yourself.

**I added someone to a date that was already sent.**
They show **Not sent**. Click **Send requests** again: only new people get an email.

**Can I schedule someone who isn't on the team?**
No. Add them to the team under **Groups** first. This keeps the list short and avoids
picking the wrong person from the whole directory.
