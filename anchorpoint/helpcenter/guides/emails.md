This guide shows you how to send an email from AnchorPoint, to one person or to a whole
group, right away or at a time you pick. Sending an email takes about **3 minutes**.

!!! note "Before you start"
    - **Your role:** You need an **Admin** login, or a login with **Can send SMS & calls**
      turned on (an Admin sets this under **Permissions**). The same permission covers
      email.
    - **Email is set up on the server.** If the Communications page says *Email isn't set
      up on the server*, ask whoever runs AnchorPoint to add the email (SMTP) settings.

## Who gets an email

AnchorPoint emails people who have **both**:

1. An **email address** on their profile, and
2. **Email opt in** ticked (on their profile it shows as *Emails: Can receive emails*).

Unlike texts, **Email opt in is on for everyone** unless they've unsubscribed. Every email
AnchorPoint sends ends with an **Unsubscribe** link. When someone clicks it, their Email
opt in turns off and they stop getting church emails.

You can also turn it off or back on by hand: open the person, click **Edit**, and tick or
untick **Email opt in**:

![The Email opt in checkbox on the person form](communications/03-email-opt-in.png)

!!! warning "Respect an unsubscribe"
    Only turn Email opt in back on when the person asks you to.

## Send an email to a group

An email to a group goes to **everyone in the group** (leaders and members) who has an
email address and hasn't unsubscribed. Each person gets their own copy, so nobody sees
anyone else's address.

1. Go to **Communications** and click **New email**.
2. Change **Send to** to **Group** and pick the group.
3. Type a **Subject**.
4. Type your **Message**. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
5. Leave **Schedule** blank to send now, or pick a date and time.
6. Click **Save & Send**.

![Emailing a whole group](communications/30-email-group.png)

You go back to the Communications page with *Email queued. It goes out within a minute.*
The email shows under **Recent emails**.

!!! tip "Writing for a team"
    Put the key detail (date, time, what changed) in the subject line. Many people only
    read the subject.

## Send an email to one person

1. Go to **Communications** and click **New email**.
2. Leave **Send to** on **Individual person**.
3. In the **Person** box, type part of their name, email or phone number, then click the
   right person. Their row turns green.
4. Type a **Subject** and **Message**.
5. Click **Save & Send**.

Pressing Enter in the search box won't send the email.

## Schedule an email for later

Pick a date and time in **Schedule** before clicking **Save & Send**. The email shows as
**Scheduled** on the Communications page and goes out within a minute of that time.
There's no cancel button for a scheduled email, so double-check it before you save.

Quiet hours (the blackout window) **don't apply to email**. An email scheduled for 10 PM
goes out at 10 PM. Texts and phone blasts still wait for quiet hours to end.

## Check what was sent

- **Communications page:** each email under **Recent emails** shows **Queued**,
  **Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Completed**, or **Failed**.
- **The person's profile:** **Communications History** lists every email sent to them,
  with the subject and the message.

**Completed** means the church's email server accepted it. AnchorPoint can't tell whether
it was opened, or whether it landed in spam.

## What people see when they unsubscribe

Clicking **Unsubscribe** opens a page that asks them to confirm. Nothing changes until
they click the button, so an email app that previews links can't unsubscribe anyone by
accident.

## Troubleshooting

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *That person does not have an email address.* | Their profile has no email. | Add their email to the profile, then try again. |
| *This person has unsubscribed from church emails.* | They clicked Unsubscribe, or staff turned Email opt in off. | Only turn it back on if they ask. |
| *Nobody in this group has an email address with emails turned on.* | No one in the group can get email. | Check the members' email addresses. |
| *Schedule time must be in the future.* | The time you picked has passed. | Pick a later time. |
| *Email isn't set up on the server…* | The server has no email settings. | Ask whoever runs AnchorPoint to add them. |
| An email shows **Failed** | The church's email server refused it. | Try again later. If it keeps failing, tell whoever runs AnchorPoint. |
| Someone says they didn't get it | It may be in their spam folder, or their address is wrong. | Ask them to check spam, then check the address on their profile. |
